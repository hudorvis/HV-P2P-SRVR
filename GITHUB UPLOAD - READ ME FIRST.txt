IMPORTANT FOR macOS FINDER
---------------------------
Finder hides folders whose names begin with a dot. This means the `.github`
folder may look as though it is missing even though it is inside this package.

Before selecting the files for a browser upload to GitHub, press:

    Command + Shift + .

That toggles hidden files on. You should then see `.github` beside `build`
and `source`.
